## 1. Kärnfrågan

The dodo is the most famous extinct animal on Earth and a company says it could walk again in the early 2030s, so why is almost everything people "know" about it wrong, and why is so little of the real bird left to build it from?

### Redan använt på kanalen (bygg INTE listan på detta)

- **Moa-videon:** det konstgjorda äggskalet (26 kycklingar ur 3D-printat skal, maj 2026) och den allmänna regeln att fåglar inte kan klonas (IUCN: "currently seems impossible"). Upprepa inte.
- **Manuset `manus/dodo-hatch-from-a-chicken.md`** (publiceringsstatus "inte bekräftat" i `manus/README.md`; kontrollera med vidIQ innan låsning). Det använder redan: "fat and stupid"-myten med ~12 kg / ~70 cm, tambalacoque-myten (Temple), utrotningsorsakerna (grisar, hundar, katter, råttor, apor), Shapiro 2002 + Nicobar-duvan, "Copenhagen genome", varför fåglar inte kan klonas, PGC-cellernas vandring i blodet, sept 2025-genombrottet (60 dagar, dubbling ~1,5 dygn), kycklingsurrogat-tricket från 1969, Mauritian Wildlife Foundation och utsättningsplanerna. Om den videon är ute: K5, K7, K21 och K26 får bara en mening var, och K24/K25 (nytt 14 okt) bär de-extinction-delen.

## 5. Använd inte

- **"The tambalacoque/dodo tree could only germinate after passing through a dodo"**: debunked (no controls in Temple's turkey test, seeds germinated without abrasion, hundreds of trees exist). Only as a myth that gets busted (K21), and the dodo-hatch script already did it.
- **The 1755 bonfire rescue** as fact. Only as the legend that turned out false (K2).
- **"Fat, clumsy, stupid"** as a description. Weight, speed and brain data say otherwise (K7–K9).
- **"Sailors called it the disgusting bird"** as a quote. "Walghvogel" means tasteless/insipid/sickly; the original 1598 Dutch journal is lost and the same account praises the belly and breast. No primary source for "disgusting" was found. Say "the Dutch named it, roughly, the tasteless bird."
- **A single exact extinction year** (1662, 1681, 1690) without hedging. 1681 (AP/NBC, Sci Am) is outdated.
- **Reuters/Daily Maverick's "up to 50 pounds / 23 kg" and "1 metre / 3 feet"** as current science.
- **Evertsz's "when we held one by the leg it cried out and the others came to help"**: not found in any source read. Do not use.
- **"Dodo" comes from Portuguese "doudo" (fool)** as settled. Origin is uncertain; Dutch "dodaars" is called more probable.
- **"Do-do-Dodgson"** as documented fact. Wikipedia calls the stammer link "popular but unsubstantiated"; OUM supports only "a dodo for the stammering Dodgson". Say "the story goes…".
- **The East London "dodo egg"** as genuine. Untested; possibly an ostrich egg (K28 only as mystery).
- **"The dodo genome was sequenced in 2002"** (Mic). 2002 was mitochondrial fragments.
- **"Colossal has a dodo embryo / dodo chick"**: nothing hatched; the 14 Oct release announced no birth.
- **"Mauritius Dodo Advisory Committee announced 14 Oct 2026"**: it was formed with the Sept 2025 announcement; the Oct 2026 release does not mention it (habitat lines come from Lamm to Reuters).
- **"The dodo will be back in 2028"**: old press timeline, superseded.
- **Shapiro's "poster child" line as from a Colossal release**: it comes from a Vice interview (via Mic), Jan/Feb 2023.
- **Ping-pong-ball size of the solitaire's knob (3.3 cm)** as precise: secondary source only.
- **"Only four dodos reached Europe"**: conflicts with Hume's ≥11 and Winters & Hume.
- **Any figure for how many chimeric chickens hatched**: no number was given.
