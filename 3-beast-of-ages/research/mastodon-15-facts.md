## 1. Kärnfrågan

The mastodon is the most common Ice Age giant in North America, it was the animal that taught the world extinction is real, and a tooth of one was fished out of a California creek in September 2026, so why does almost everything about it, from its name to its tusks to who found it first, make no sense, and why does nobody want to bring it back?

## 5. Använd inte

- **A mastodon lifespan of "50–60 years"**: no source. Only individual ages (Fred 34, Burning Tree ~30).
- **A mastodon gestation length**: no mastodon-specific figure; the 22 months is elephant/mammoth. Write around it.
- **"Mastodons made the Sauk Trail / a Michigan Mastodon Trail"**: one blog repost; no paper or museum source.
- **"Mastodons had reddish-brown hair"**: Eiseley 1946 shows the claim rests on repetition; the 1991 Milwaukee samples give no colour.
- **Hunter's "as men we cannot but thank Heaven" as a quotation**: not verified verbatim.
- **"Jefferson ordered Lewis and Clark to bring back a live mastodon"**: Monticello says "search for"; lewis-clark.org is weaker. Hedge.
- **"The bones were laid out in the East Room"**: no source names the room; say "the President's House".
- **A piano, toasts or songs at the ribcage dinner**: not found.
- **"The last mastodons died in the Great Lakes 10,500 cal BP"**: not found; use the Indiana dates.
- **Monte Verde (Chile) "mastodon"**: a gomphothere, not *Mammut*.
- **"The 2026 tooth is 10,000 years old"** as fact: estimate, carbon dating pending; Thompson said 50,000–100,000.
- **Colossal at a $20–30 billion valuation (TechCrunch, July 2026)**: seen only in a remix video's description; not verified.
- **Facts Machine's "~11,500 years ago" as our own figure**: use dated specimens.
- **"Enterobacter cloacae" from the Burning Tree gut**: not confirmed; say "dozens of living bacterial species".
- **"The Michigan trackway is the only mastodon trackway ever found"** (one museum page): conflicts with "largest and most complete".
- **"The first mounted fossil skeleton in the world"**: Peale's was the first in America, second anywhere (Madrid *Megatherium*).
- **"Cuvier discovered extinction with the mastodon alone"**: he used mammoth and elephants too; Hunter had argued extinction in 1768.
- **The band Mastodon, the Mastodon social network**: not this video.
- **"Mastodon" = "mammoth" in any sentence**: K8 exists precisely to separate them.
