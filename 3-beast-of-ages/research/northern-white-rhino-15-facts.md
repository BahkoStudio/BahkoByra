## 1. Kärnfrågan

How can a species with only two animals left, both female and neither able to carry a calf, still have a real chance of being born again?

### Redan använt på kanalen (bygg INTE listan på detta)

Enligt uppdragsgivaren nämndes den nordliga vita noshörningen en gång i rankningsvideon (Ipsos 2021: 23 % av amerikanerna ville ha den tillbaka, plats två efter jättesköldpaddor med 26 %; Ipsos, sept 2021, ipsos.com/en-us/node/810456). Obs: nuvarande `manus/which-extinct-animals-worth-bringing-back-ranked.md` innehåller bara Ipsos-siffrorna för dodo (22 %), mammut (19 %) och "ingen" (30 %), inte noshörningen. Använd inte Ipsos-siffran igen.
Annars inget om Najin, Fatu, Sudan eller BioRescue på kanalen. Kanalens generella de-extinction-material (Colossal, dire wolf, Seddon 2014) får bara nämnas i en rad (K26).

## 5. Använd inte

- **"Fatu was shot dead by a sniper" (22 May 2026)**: only TechBullion (tech writer Anamta Shehzadi) and crypto-exchange MEXC news pages (now 410 Gone), both citing an unlinked X post attributed to caretaker Peter Esegon. No Ol Pejeta, BioRescue, KWS, Reuters, AP or BBC confirmation found. Treat as false until an official source says otherwise. If it is ever confirmed, the whole script changes: check first (§6).
- **"Sudan was the last male rhino in the world"**: he was the last male *northern white*. Black and southern white males exist.
- **"Rhino horn is worth more than gold / cocaine"**: no dated source. UNODC 2024 says the US$65,000–100,000/kg figures were popular in 2014–16 and the 2019 price was about US$16,000/kg. Use only the UNODC numbers, with years.
- **Inflated guard numbers** ("40 armed guards around each rhino"): BioRescue FAQ's "42 armed rangers" and "120 rhino patrol rangers" are undated and cover the whole conservancy. Say "armed guards around the clock".
- **"They were dehorned to stop poachers"**: only an undated Anadolu piece from Sudan's time. The 2025 fact is that rangers trim Fatu's horn so she doesn't hurt Najin (K23).
- **"Nabire's eggs were fertilised with southern sperm by mistake"**: only in Wikipedia, not found in BioRescue or Safari Park sources.
- **The *wyd*/"wide mouth" origin of "white"** as a fact (K18 debunks it).
- **"Najin's legs were ruined by concrete floors"**: one outlet, via sökutdrag only.
- **Teaser bull's name "Ouwan"**: seen only in a sökutdrag of a Fox syndication; not on the pages read.
- **Projections presented as facts**: "calves by early 2026", "two to three years", "three to four years". None came true by Oct 2026.
- **Space Daily (10 Sep 2026)** as a source: column with no named author that recalculates the consortium's numbers itself. It shows nothing new happened, but cite the primary sources.
- **Spam pages with "2026" in the title** (face.meei.harvard.edu, lsfellowship.missouri.edu, ir.mountaintopuniversity.edu.ng, acls-aatc.ca): machine-generated, not sources.
- **"Sudan's Google Doodle"** (Wikipedia, 20 Dec 2020): not checked against Google; skip.
- **Colossal's "January 2026 Short"**: not found. Don't refer to it.
- **Ipsos 2021 (23 %)**: already on the channel.
- **Kes Hillman Smith's "Suni died in 2016"**: error; it was 2014.
