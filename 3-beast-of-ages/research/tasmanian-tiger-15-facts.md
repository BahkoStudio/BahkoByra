## 1. Kärnfrågan

What was actually so strange about the Tasmanian tiger that, ninety years after the last one died, it still breaks the rules of what a predator, a marsupial and even an extinct animal are supposed to be?

### Redan använt på kanalen (bygg INTE listan på detta)

Natten 7 sept 1936 och dödsfallet i Hobart-zoot · Benjamin-namnet och könsdebatten · 2 184 statliga skottpengar och £1 per huvud · skyddet 59 dagar före döden · käkarna för svaga för får (Attard 2011, Weisbecker 2026) · 1 237 / 3 000 observationer och Brook et al. 2023 · Colossals 99,9 % genom, fettsvansad pungmus (dunnart) som surrogat, risgrynsstora ungar, 300 redigeringar, målet 2036 · utdöd på fastlandet för ~3 000 år sedan och hällmålningarna i Arnhem Land (inkl. Taçon 2026, 14 nya målningar) · toppredatorns ekologi · Seddon 2014.
Gränsfall: kandidat 26 (kvarlevorna i TMAG 2022) och kandidat 14 (Fleays film) gränsar till 1936-natten. Använd bara de delar som står i tabellerna.

## 5. Använd inte

- **"Benjamin"** as the animal's real name: traced to Frank Darby in 1968, a man with no record of working at the zoo; Paddle calls it a fabrication. Already covered by the channel anyway.
- **"The keeper locked it out that night" / "it froze to death"**: only secondary retellings (Smithsonian, vintage-news sites); the Tasmanian government says "exposure, due to suspected neglect". Neither the mechanism nor a named keeper is backed by records. Do not use.
- **"Jaws opened 120° / 180°"**: Guinness 2018 says no wider than 80°. Use only as a myth that gets busted (K20).
- **"It drank only blood" / vampire killer of sheep**: no reliable source found.
- **"Could not wag its tail"**: only on a hobby site (trishansoz.com). Say "stiff tail" (NRE Tas).
- **Bipedal kangaroo hop**: only encyclopaedia copies and spam mirrors ("demonstrated at various times by captive specimens", Guiler's speculation), no primary source read. Use only as "witnesses claimed…", or skip it.
- **"An 1863 female jumped 6–8 feet to the cage rafters"**: unsourced conservation page.
- **Ted Turner's amount ($100,000)**: amount not seen in a primary or news source; year and setting are fine.
- **"It bred in captivity once (Melbourne 1899)"** as an absolute claim: weak sourcing, conflicts with the state government.
- **"Last thylacine in America"**: no reliable source for who or when.
- **Any 2026 video of a "live thylacine"** (YouTube shorts, the Bothwell August 2026 story): unverified.
- **"Colossal sequenced the entire genome in early 2025"** (June 2026 explainer): incorrect; the 99.9% genome is from Oct 2024 and already used by the channel.
- **The Clownfish TV/D-REZZED article (2026-10-10)**: aggregator with AI disclosure, unsourced figures ("20 edits", "8 years"). Not a source.
- **Soundcy's claim that a 1905 Hobart sound recording exists**: false; no recordings exist.
- **Oxford "100 specimens" detail**: secondary only. The exam anecdote itself is OK (Nat Geo 2011, attributed to Dawkins).
- **"Dog-faced dasyurus"** as a name: not found.
